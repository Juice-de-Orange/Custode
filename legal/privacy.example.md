## What is processed

Account and household data that you enter (for example recipes, tasks, notes, appointments),
sign-in data (sessions, passkeys, two-factor settings) and a sign-in log that records the
country of a sign-in (if the proxy in front supplies it) but never the IP address. Vault
contents are encrypted on your device; the server only stores ciphertext.

Optional features, only if [the operator enables them / you use them]: wearable data from Oura
(health data, only with your consent), weather for your location (Open-Meteo), quick-capture with
an LLM, calendar sync with an external CalDAV server, e-mail (verification, password reset,
weekly digest).

## Purposes and legal bases

- Providing the household features and syncing between your devices — performance of a contract
  (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Health data from wearables — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can
  disconnect the wearable at any time in the app; its data is then erased.
- Security and abuse prevention — legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

No advertising, no sale of data.
